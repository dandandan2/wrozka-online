Roadmap S-04, last slice of milestone M-1. Users can view their full
fairy_responses history, delete an entry (which also removes it from
S-03's style-learning pool for free via hard delete), and like/unlike an
entry from the history list. Requires S-01 (ask flow) and S-03 (style
pool, so there's something for deletion to meaningfully affect).
